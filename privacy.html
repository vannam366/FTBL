Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Information we collect

When you register and use interactive features, we process your username, email, encrypted account credentials, avatar selection, comments, likes, favorites, reports, block relationships, followed teams or leagues, and Widget preferences.

How we use it

The information is used to sign you in, sync your personal content, display comments, enforce content rules, generate personalized match snapshots, and keep the service secure.

Content moderation

Comments pass through content filtering. Reports are used for review, and block relationships hide comments from users you block.

Account deletion

You can start a permanent deletion from Me, then Account Settings, then Delete Account. Your account and associated interaction data are handled by the server-side deletion process.
