Technical Support

Help for accounts, football data, news, and the iOS Widget.

Account and sign-in

If you cannot sign in, use the password reset link from the Sign In screen. To change your username, open Me, then Account Settings, then edit the username field.

News and comments

Published news appears in the News tab. Use the report action on an article or comment to flag content for review.

Widget

The large iOS Widget uses the latest cached snapshot written by the app. Open the app and save the Widget snapshot again if it appears outdated.
